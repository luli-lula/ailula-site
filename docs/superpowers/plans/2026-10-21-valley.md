# Valley Diorama (Hi-Fi Home) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (chosen: native, unattended overnight run). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build the Hi-Fi home: a stylized low-poly valley on a floating island, with a giant sequoia at its center. Each section of the site is a clickable place in the valley. The valley has season and day/night toggles, and it uses real site content in both languages.

**Architecture:** Pure data modules (palette, zones) are unit-tested. Scene builders in `src/scenes/valley/` produce merged low-poly meshes. `createValley()` owns the renderer, controls, labels, fly-to and toggles, and emits zone selections. `Valley.astro` is the Hi-Fi overlay UI (top bar, toggles, content panels). It is rendered on `/` and `/zh/`, and its script is dynamically imported only when `html[data-mode="hifi"]`. The Lo-Fi page underneath is unchanged.

**Tech Stack:** Astro 7, Three.js r186 (OrbitControls, CSS2DRenderer, BufferGeometryUtils), GSAP for camera tweens, Vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-redwood-site-design.md`, the section "最终方向（2026-10-21 决定…）"

## Global Constraints

- **Never invent content.** Missing facts use `待补充 / TODO`. Panels show only data from collections, `src/data/timeline.ts` and `src/data/profile.ts`.
- **Costs and side effects:** no paid assets, no deploy, no push, no repo creation. Do not touch `luli-lula.github.io` or `douban-data`.
- **Botany:**
  - The giant sequoia (Sequoiadendron giganteum) is evergreen: its crown colour is the same in every season.
  - Seasonal change is carried by Pacific dogwood (white bracts in spring, green in summer, red in autumn, bare in winter), the ground and the sky.
  - Other conifers are white fir and sugar pine shapes.
- **Composition:** the sequoia must dominate. It is at least 2.5× the height of any other tree, and its trunk is clearly the thickest object.
- **Look:** flat-shaded low-poly, muted palette, soft shadows, no photo textures.
- **Existing behaviour must keep working:** Lo-Fi, i18n, redirects and the existing tests must stay green.
- **Performance:** at most 120 draw calls on desktop. Mobile (≤700px) uses dpr 1, no shadows and fewer particles.

## Review Focus

1. **No WebGL.** The home stays fully usable in Lo-Fi and the valley script never runs.
2. **Phone, 375px wide, Hi-Fi.** The top bar and panels fit without horizontal scroll. Touch orbit works, and panels can be closed.
3. **Switching language from the valley.** The page lands on `/zh/` in Hi-Fi with the same season and day/night.
4. **Rapid zone clicks.** Camera tweens do not stack or leave the camera inside the tree.
5. **Content links in panels.** They go to existing Lo-Fi routes (`/projects/<slug>/`, `/writing/<slug>/`).

### Task 2: The giant sequoia

**Files:**
- Create: `src/scenes/valley/sequoia.ts`
- Create: `src/scenes/valley/lowpoly.ts` (helpers: seeded rng, jitter, merge-by-material)

**Interfaces:**
- `createSequoia(p: Palette): { group: THREE.Group; height: number; hollowAnchor: THREE.Vector3; ringHeights: number[]; setPalette(p: Palette): void }`

**Shape:**
- A fluted, cinnamon-red trunk that is broad at the base, with a buttressed flare. It tapers slowly and only gently.
- A dark fire-scar cavity at the base facing the camera. This is the cinema entrance and mirrors the Grizzly Giant.
- A few thick limbs high up that bend upward, carrying large rounded clumps of foliage (sequoia crown).
- Faint ring bands on the trunk at each timeline year's height.

### Task 4: createValley — renderer, camera, labels, toggles

**Files:**
- Create: `src/scenes/valley/createValley.ts`

**Interface:**
- `createValley(canvas: HTMLCanvasElement, labelsEl: HTMLElement, opts: { lang: Lang; quality: 'high' | 'low' }) => { setSeason(s: Season): void; setNight(n: boolean): void; focus(id: ZoneId | null): void; onSelect(cb: (id: ZoneId) => void): void; ready: Promise<void>; dispose(): void }`

**Behaviour:**
- OrbitControls with damping, a limited polar angle and zoom, and a slow idle auto-rotate.
- Labels are CSS2D pill buttons at the zone anchors.
- Clicking a label or a zone object calls `onSelect`.
- `focus()` tweens the camera with GSAP and kills any running tween first (Review Focus 4).
- Selecting `tree` plays a slow rise along the trunk past the ring bands.

**Steps:**

- [ ] E2E test `tests/e2e/valley-lab.spec.ts`: the canvas renders, there are 6 labels, and clicking "Workshop" sets `data-zone="workshop"` on `<html>`. No console errors.
- [ ] Commit.
