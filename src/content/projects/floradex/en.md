Pokédex for plants: an iOS app for collecting and organising the plants you have met, like Letterboxd, but for plants.

Import from iNaturalist or scan photos to collect plants. Each species becomes a holographic card that shimmers as you tilt the phone (a Metal shader driven by the gyroscope), and a family and genus treemap shows how much of the plant world you have collected.

In development; not released yet.
