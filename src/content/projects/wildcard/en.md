An iOS app: photograph a plant and get a knowledge card for it.

It cross-checks AI identifications against local flora using GPS location and maps each user's finds with a spatial index. Built with Swift, SwiftUI, Core ML and multimodal LLMs.

Feature-complete; preparing for App Store submission.
