Paper review and reference checking: upload an ML paper, and every claim is located in the literature and every citation verified.

Built on the FactReview paper (Yue et al., 2026, [arXiv:2604.04074](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04074)).

My part was the product. The paper came with only a GitHub repository; I turned it into a website anyone can use, and added a way for users to flag results that are wrong, so their feedback feeds back into the tool.
